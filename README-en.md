## 👨‍💻 About me

I work at **TOTVS** as a Service Analyst on the Prime Support team, providing specialized support within the Protheus ecosystem (Tax Reform, Smart View, and modules for Billing, Inventory/Costs, Purchasing, and PCP).

Day to day I work a lot with **ADVPL and TL++ (TLPP)**, databases, and corporate systems administration. In the PHP world, **Laravel** is the framework I know best and use as my main back-end reference.

I use GitHub as a personal lab — study projects, automations, and experiments that don't fit into my day job.
